03 / THE SAFE BAND CONCEPT

Subtle on the wrist.
Ready by design.

SafeBand is being developed as a personal safety wearable for women, men, children and older people. The design direction favours a minimal band silhouette, an edge-facing camera and a flush SOS button.

  • Soft-touch band direction
  • Integrated side camera concept
  • Low-profile tactile SOS control
  • Companion app experience
